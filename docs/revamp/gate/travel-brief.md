## Mechanism

Three mechanisms were considered: restoring the original two-plane slit scan, expanding
the neighboring particle warp, and an offset slit canyon. The canyon keeps the original
Gate's sunset color source while making actual continuous forward travel visible. It
adds broad projected surfaces and staggered, open, one-sided fins that pass the camera.
It avoids duplicating Jump's particles and Rings' repeated closed hoops.

The canvas is the environment, not a picture of an object. A real depth projection places
crisp wall, floor and ceiling ribbons from the distant seam to every viewport edge.
World stations move toward and behind the viewer, then wrap in the distance. Saturated
amber, coral and rose sample the already-provenanced Sonoran sunset strip, with authored
cyan tracks and near-black structural seams. No appliance silhouette or circular iris.

Base motion glides forward continuously. Boost rises, surges and settles into the same
forward glide without reversing the camera or returning to an earlier object. Calm
reduces speed and energy. Still selects an authored travel composition, with a synchronous
alternate frame for Boost and no ticker. Native scrolling, Auto-fly interruption, sound
intent and remembered mute, exports, provenance and one host ticker remain contracts.

## Review before release

Inspect a complete normal-speed journey on phone first, then desktop: cruise, Boost,
settlement, interruption and repeat. Stills demonstrate composition only. Observe
identifiable fins emerge from depth, grow and pass beyond the screen edges; glowing or
cycling color alone is insufficient. Near passes should sustain a cinematic rhythm
without flicker, wraps or a reverse return. Portrait fills the viewport and keeps its
vanishing seam above the control plate, with fewer, thicker readable paths.

Technical review separately checks actual keyboard and touch controls, Still PNG/HTML,
Calm, audio event order, cancellation, resize and destroy/remount. Measure synchronous
module work at normal requestAnimationFrame cadence, targeting under 4ms mean. This
excludes GPU, compositor, paint and physical-device thermal behavior. Owner acceptance
remains open until the owner responds to the exact replacement build.
