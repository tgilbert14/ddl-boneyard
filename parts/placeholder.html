How it works

A radial gradient in the phosphor colour sits on the shared vanishing point and breathes by breathe around its base alpha over period seconds. A hairline crosses the frame at the vanishing point's height, double-stroked. A bezel rectangle is inset by bezel of the shorter side. still() draws the same frame at mid-breath.

Provenance

technique
the designed idle of a switched-on vector tube: a centre bloom on the vanishing point, one horizontal hairline that breathes, an inset bezel; nothing else
lineage
the idle screen of a vector CRT with no program loaded (the oscilloscope at rest; Vectrex, 1982, between games)
original
